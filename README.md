The project involves predicting gold prices in dollars per ounce in Azerbaijan for the last one decade from 2012-2022.
Data sources are based purely online through investment.com and other public datasets mined through annual data reports.
Four variables have been used with emphasis on prices of gold in Azerbaijan measured in dollars; annual inflation rates of Azerbaijan as a percentage; Annual inflation rates of Azerbaijan (%), and the USD/AZN exchange rates.
Analysis included time series modelling and forecasting, descriptive statistics, Pearson's bivariate analysis, and Linear regression Analyses.
